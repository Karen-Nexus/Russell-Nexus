Privacy and Cookies Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to our website. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website.

🏢 1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Russell Nexus Limited ("we", "us", or "our").

📊 2. Information We Collect (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our content and user experience. Google Analytics works by placing small text files called "cookies" on your device.

The types of data tracked include:

  • Which pages you visit on our site
  • How long you spend on each page
  • The type of web browser and device you are using
  • Your approximate geographic location (e.g., city level)

This data is completely anonymised. We cannot identify you personally from this information.

🍪 3. How We Handle Cookie Consent

In compliance with privacy laws (including the UK/EU GDPR), we operate an "opt-in" policy for tracking cookies:

  • If you click "Accept" on our cookie banner, Google Analytics cookies will load and help us track website usage statistics.
  • If you click "Decline" or ignore the banner, Google Analytics cookies are blocked entirely, and no tracking data is sent to Google.

❌ 4. How to Opt-Out Permanently

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's cookies, which will cause our cookie banner to reappear.

Alternatively, if you want to block Google Analytics across all websites you visit on the internet, you can download and install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
